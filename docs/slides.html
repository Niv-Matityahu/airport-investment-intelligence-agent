The job

Help a fund pick where to build

The firm invests in airport modernization. It needs to know which airports a terminal or runway build will make most productive — and to ask in plain English.

  • "Which New England airports are strong expansion candidates?"
  • "Compare LA and Santa Ana congestion."
  • "% long-haul out of Anchorage?"
  • "Unmet demand at SFO — and why?"
The insight most candidates miss

Big & growing isn't enough — it must be choked

the differentiator

🚦 Choke

Jammed: delays, ~100% runway use, or FAA flight caps. If it has room, no build is needed.

📈 Demand

Traffic is growing, so new capacity gets used.

🏗️ Scale

Big enough to justify a major build.

A huge airport with spare room just absorbs growth. Choke is what turns a build into profit — so the whole score is built around it.

The scoring logic (deterministic, not the LLM)

One number: the Expansion Opportunity Score

EOS = ( Choke 45% + Demand 30% + Scale 25% ) × Choke-gate

The gate = 0.60 + 0.40 × choke shrinks the score when an airport isn't jammed — so scale + growth alone can't reach the top.

Tradeoffs & what's next

Honest about the edges

  • Demand = one year of growth — noisy; a multi-year trend / FAA forecast is the first upgrade.
  • No gate/seat data exists publicly — capacity proxied by runways + slot-control + utilization.
  • Delays = 3 sampled months, domestic-only — understates international gateways.
  • Snapshot is CY2024 — refresh on a schedule for production.

All weights + the gate floor live in config.py — tunable, not buried.
